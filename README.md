# Aung Min Thu - Forklift Operator

## Biography
Hello, I’m Aung Min Thu. I am a **Forklift Operator** at **Mama Noodles Factory** (Ayeyarwaddy Food Industry Co., Ltd.) with over 15 years of experience in the **finished goods department**. I began working at the factory on **October 22, 2008**, and my role involves operating forklifts to efficiently and safely move finished products within the warehouse.

I was born on **April 29, 1990**, in the **Ayeyarwaddy Region**. My parents are **U Myint Thein** and **Daw Aye Khaing**. I got married in **2015** to **Thu Zar Myint**, and we currently live in **Padan Yekyaw Village, Twantay Township**.

## My Work and Projects
- **Forklift Operations**: Managing the movement and storage of finished products using forklifts to ensure smooth workflow in the warehouse.
- **Efficiency Improvement**: Looking for ways to improve forklift operation efficiency, ensuring that products are transported safely and quickly.
- **Safety**: Focused on maintaining high safety standards in the warehouse by following best practices in forklift operation.
- **Documentation & Record Keeping**: Maintaining daily logs and product tracking records with accuracy.

## Future Goals
My goal is to enhance the efficiency and safety of forklift operations in the finished goods department. I aim to streamline warehouse processes and improve the overall workflow through smarter solutions and disciplined operations.
